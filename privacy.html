Privacy

A plain-English note on what this site collects and why.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Who I am

This site belongs to Ellen Ivanovic. For anything about your data, email hello@ellenivanovic.com.

What I collect

Only what you type into the workshop waitlist form: your first name, email, the sessions you’re interested in, whether you’re joining for yourself or a group, your organisation name if you give one, the date you signed up and your consent to be emailed.

Why

To email you when dates are set for the workshops you picked. Nothing else: no newsletter, no selling or sharing your details, no marketing from anyone else.

Where it’s stored

In a private Google Sheet in my own Google account, which only I can open. Google stores it on its servers, and its privacy policy applies to that storage. I also get an email with the details of each new sign-up.

How long I keep it

Until you ask me to remove you, or until the workshop series ends, whichever comes first.

How to be removed

Reply to any email from me or email hello@ellenivanovic.com and ask to be removed. I’ll delete your details and confirm. You can also ask to see or correct what I hold about you.

The rest of the site

This site uses no tracking or advertising cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to fetch them. The newsletter is run by Substack and their privacy policy applies when you subscribe there.
