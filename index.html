What I share

Where AI actually helps in your day-to-day and where it doesn’t. I share the prompts and skills I use for everyday tasks like emails, reporting and content, so you can try them on your own work.

When you’re ready for the next step, I show you how to hand the tasks you repeat every week to simple automations in n8n and Claude, and I share the workflows I’ve built.

I build AI products in public and share every step: user research and feedback, product development, go-to-market and, last but not least, safety and evals, the part most people skip. You get what worked, what broke and how I fixed it.

I speak at events and on podcasts about AI tools at work and using AI to upskill and get job-ready. Now I run beginner-friendly AI workshops too.

Break Your Build

👈

* Products and people get better the same way. You find where they break, you fix it, and you build them back stronger.

Break your product

Look honestly at what you’re building. Test it with real people, push it until it breaks and find the weak spots before your users do. Then fix what’s missing and build it back stronger.

Break yourself down

Do the same with yourself. Look honestly at your skills, your strengths, your weaknesses and even your flaws. If you feel stuck, or lost in where your work is going while AI changes everything around it, find what’s missing, learn what you need and build yourself back up stronger.
